Who I am

Hi! I am Chloe Cheuk, and I am currently a freshman at the University of Pennsylvania, where I plan on studying Design and Computer Science with a minor in Consumer Psychology.

I come from a traditional fine arts background and have been doing art for most of my life. During my sophomore year of high school, I decided to pivot into design because I realized I could directly impact groups in need by utilizing a human-first perspective in my design work.

That led me to do pro-bono design consulting for over 75 local businesses, where I did everything from marketing and website design to brand design. I also started the first-ever design club at my high school, where I hosted design directors and workshops. Beyond my school, I designed for national student organizations such as CLA and JSA. I also created my own personal projects, such as Sebio and Blood on Dot.
