Who I am

Hi! This is Chloe Cheuk, and I’m currently a freshman at the University of Pennsylvania where I plan on studying Design and Computer Science with a minor in Consumer Psychology.

I come from a traditional fine arts background, and after doing arts for all of my life in sophomore year of high school I decided to pivot into design because I realized I could directly impact groups in need by utilizing a human-first perspective in my design works.

That led me to do pro-bono design consulting over 75 local businesses, where I did anything from marketing, website, to brand design; start the first ever design club at my high school, hosting design directors and workshops; design for national student organizations such as CLA and JSA; and create my own personal projects such as Sebio and Blood on Dot.
